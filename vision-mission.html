Our Vision

To build a financially inclusive society where every citizen has access to formal financial services, enabling them to participate in and benefit from India's economic growth, while ensuring the stability and efficiency of the financial system.

Our Mission

Universal Financial Access

Ensure every Indian has access to basic banking services through innovative delivery channels and technology-driven solutions.

Strengthen Financial Institutions

Build robust banks and insurance companies that can support India's economic aspirations while maintaining financial stability.

Digital Transformation

Promote digital banking and fintech innovations to make financial services more accessible, efficient, and secure.

Financial Literacy

Enhance financial awareness and literacy to empower citizens to make informed financial decisions and protect their interests.

Consumer Protection

Safeguard consumer interests through robust grievance redressal mechanisms and fair practices in financial services.

Sustainable Growth

Foster sustainable and inclusive economic growth by channeling credit to productive sectors and supporting entrepreneurship.
